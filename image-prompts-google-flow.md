## Tips for Flow
- Generate 2 to 4 variations per prompt and pick the cleanest one (watch for warped hands and odd shapes on equipment).
- If Flow adds readable text on bottles or signs, add: "all labels blank".
- Export around 1600 px on the long side for 16:9 and 1200 px on the long side for 4:3 and 3:4, to keep the page fast.
- Send the finished images back here and I'll remove the watermark and drop them into the page.
