About us

Cold Lake's choice for janitorial services

At Cold Lake Janitorial, we're committed to providing our services in an affordable, courteous, professional and efficient manner. We've been diligently serving our commercial and residential clients since 1996, and we've built a reputation for being Cold Lake's carpet cleaning specialist.

We are also pleased to offer our services 24-hours a day, 7 days a week and we also offer emergency appointments for our clientele. From ceramic tile steam cleaning to scheduled office janitorial services, we can handle your cleaning needs expediently. Our commitment to client satisfaction and expedient services has made us Cold Lake's choice for janitorial services.
